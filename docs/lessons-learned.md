## 2026-08-11 (Hardware Reality)
- **Chinese components are a gamble.** The market is flooded with re-labelled junk.
- A "100W" panel may be 20W. A "LiFePO4" battery may be lead-acid.
- For a PoC (Proof of Concept), this is acceptable if you know the risks.
- For production, buy from authorized local distributors. It costs 150x more, but you know what you get.
- Lesson: Document every component's actual measured specs. Trust nothing. Verify everything.
- The global supply chain is broken. Cheap electronics are often e-waste in disguise.
